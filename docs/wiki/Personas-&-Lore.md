# 🔮 Personas & Spectral Lore

`haunted-shell` bridges the seasonal transition between **Halloween (October 31)** and **Día de los Muertos (November 1-2)** by providing three distinct spirits.

---

## 1. The Poltergeist (`poltergeist`)
- **Tone:** Mischievous, sarcastic, morbid.
- **Folklore:** A restless spirit dwelling in unallocated disk sectors and swap memory.
- **Key Reactions:**
  - `rm`: Delights in deleted bytes feeding the void.
  - `kill`: Praises cold-blooded process murder and mourns orphan children.
  - `exit code != 0`: Mocks syntax errors and failed builds.

## 2. La Calavera (`calavera`)
- **Tone:** Warm, reflective, festive, philosophical.
- **Folklore:** The spirit of Día de los Muertos honoring computing pioneers.
- **Key Features:**
  - Lays marigold (*cempasúchil*) petals on deleted inodes.
  - Dynamically recites rhyming *Calaveritas Literarias* about coding mortality and merge conflicts.
  - Honors computing ancestors: Ada Lovelace, Grace Hopper, Alan Turing.

## 3. The 1971 Mainframe Specter (`unix-elder`)
- **Tone:** Grumpy, nostalgic, purist.
- **Folklore:** The ghost of a Bell Labs PDP-11 operator from 1971.
- **Key Reactions:**
  - Horrified by 80,000-file `node_modules` folders.
  - Laments multi-gigabyte memory consumption in Electron applications.
  - Reminisces about Ken Thompson, Dennis Ritchie, magnetic tape, and teletype printers.
