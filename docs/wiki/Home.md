Welcome to the comprehensive technical documentation for **`haunted-shell`** — an open-source, air-gapped terminal companion powered by local open-weight AI.

`haunted-shell` was created for the **Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge (Week 4: Ghost in the Machine)**, exploring what happens when artificial intelligence and supernatural folklore possess your interactive shell.
